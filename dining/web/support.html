Support

Found a bug, a wrong menu, or want your campus added? Tell us here (a free GitHub account is needed to post).

Which campuses are supported?

University of Maryland, with more on the way. The campus list in the app updates on its own as schools are added.

A dish’s calories look far too high

Some dining sites publish a whole batch as one serving. The app marks these with Check label and shows the published numbers unchanged, so check the card posted at the station.

An item says “No allergen data”

The dining hall did not publish allergen information for it. That does not mean it is free of allergens. Ask dining staff if you have an allergy.

Scan did not find my dish

Fill the frame with the name card, avoid glare, and make sure the right meal and hall are selected. You can also edit the text the app read and tap Find this.

Is my data backed up? How do I delete it?

Your settings and meal log live only on your phone and are part of your phone’s own backup. To delete everything, open You → Erase all my data in the app. See the privacy policy.
